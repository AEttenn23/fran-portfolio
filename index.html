Skills

I'm a Concept Artist and Character Artist based in Valencia, Spain. My work moves between 2D and 3D: from concept sketches and color studies to sculpted, textured, game-ready characters and hero props. I love anatomy, likeness and hair, and I'm always pushing to make every asset cleaner and more believable.

Professional skills

  • Game-ready characters and props, from concept to final renders
  • Digital sculpting of anatomy, creatures and likeness in ZBrush
  • Realtime hair with hair cards: strand flow, layering and silhouette
  • PBR texturing in Substance 3D Painter, including painted and chipped metal
  • Clean low/mid-poly modelling, UV layouts and baking
  • Blendshapes for facial expressions
  • Concept art, character design and illustration
  • Strong grounding in color theory, values and greyscale studies
  • Studio experience in an outsourcing pipeline (Hunting Souls)
