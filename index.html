Skills

I'm a 3D Character Artist based in Valencia, Spain, specializing in realistic and stylized characters for games. I take characters from concept to game-ready production: modeling, sculpting, retopology, texturing, grooming and optimization, with hands-on experience in real-time pipelines and Unreal Engine.

Professional skills

  • Production-ready real-time characters, realistic and stylized, from concept to final asset
  • High-resolution digital sculpting of anatomy, creatures and likeness in ZBrush
  • Retopology, character topology and optimized UV layouts
  • PBR texturing, from realistic skin and worn metal to stylized materials
  • Character grooming: hair cards, fur and feathers with FiberShop and Blender
  • Realistic clothing with Marvelous Designer
  • Realistic characters with Unreal Engine and MetaHuman
  • Game-ready props for production and external clients
  • Real-time optimization and preparing assets for production pipelines
  • Concept art for characters and environments
  • Studio experience with international teams, fluent communication in English
